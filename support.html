Frequently asked questions

How do I turn a photo into a bead pattern?

On the home screen, tap Photo to Pattern, pick an image from your library, and crop it to a 1:1 square. Choose the pattern size and how many colors to use, then generate. You can follow the on-board guide bead by bead, or fill the whole pattern in a single tap. Tip: high-contrast photos with simple composition produce clearer patterns.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Creating, editing, and exporting all work fully offline. A connection is only used for optional features such as watching a rewarded video or making an in-app purchase.

Where are my creations stored?

Everything is stored locally on your device — your artworks, drafts, bead balances, and settings. Nothing is uploaded to a server, and your photos are processed on-device only.

What are beads and how do I get more?

Beads are virtual items used to place beads while you create. You receive a set of beads to start. You can top up by watching an optional rewarded video or with a one-time in-app purchase. If you prefer to create without limits, a single "Permanent Unlimited" unlock is available.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. Non-consumable purchases (such as Permanent Unlimited) will be restored to your device.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Please request refunds through Apple's official channels at reportaproblem.apple.com. We are unable to process App Store refunds directly.

Can I save or share my artwork?

Yes. You can export a shareable image of any piece. When you save to your photo library, the app requests permission to add the image — it does not read other photos in your library.

Is Bead Planet suitable for children?

Bead Planet is intended for hobbyists of all ages and is suitable for parents and children to create together. It is not intended for standalone use by children under 13. We recommend guardians enable Apple's "Ask to Buy" and "Screen Time" to manage in-app purchases. Note: making physical bead crafts (for example, using an iron to fuse beads) should always be done under adult supervision.
