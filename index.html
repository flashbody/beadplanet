What you can do

Everything you need to design, plan, and share pixel bead creations.

🎨

Create from scratch

Tap through a 48-color palette of glossy, donut-style beads. Choose a board size, then place, erase, and recolor freely on a tidy grid.

🖼️

Photo to Pattern

Pick a photo, crop it 1:1, and get a bead pattern automatically. Follow the on-board guide bead by bead, or fill the whole pattern in a single tap.

📋

Material list

See exactly how many beads of each color your design needs before you start, so you can prepare your real-world supplies with confidence.

💾

Project management

Save drafts and continue later, keep a gallery of finished pieces, and export a clean, shareable image of any creation.

🔒

Privacy first

Runs fully on your device. Your photos are processed locally and never uploaded. No account required. Optional ads respect your privacy choices.

🌍

English & Chinese

Friendly, tactile design with thoughtful onboarding, available in English and Chinese, so you can make your first piece in minutes.
