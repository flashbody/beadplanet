Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026 · Applies to: Bead Planet (豆趣星球) iOS App

Thank you for using Bead Planet ("the App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information. Please read it carefully before use.

1. Core Principle: Local-First Data

The App is a fully local application. All of your creative data — including bead artworks, drafts, bead balances, favorites, and settings — is stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to any server, and we cannot access it.

2. How We Handle Information

2.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email, or ID documents.
  • We do not collect the content of your artworks.
  • We operate no server that stores any of your data.

2.2 Information Stored Locally

The following is stored only on your device to enable app functionality and never leaves your device:

  • Your bead artworks, drafts, and thumbnails;
  • Bead balances, bucket data, and purchase status;
  • Favorites, learning records, and app settings (e.g., dark mode, language);
  • Optional local nickname and avatar.

2.3 Information Processed by Third Parties

(1) In-App Purchases (Apple)
The App uses Apple's App Store In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Transactions are handled by Apple; we only receive an anonymous transaction receipt to grant virtual items. Payment information is managed by Apple; we never access your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

(2) Advertising (if enabled)
The App may include rewarded video ads from third-party ad providers (e.g., Google AdMob). To comply with child-protection requirements, ads are configured as non-personalized, and providers are instructed not to perform child-directed behavioral targeting. Ad providers may collect limited information such as device identifiers for ad delivery and fraud prevention. See the respective ad provider's privacy policy.

(3) Sign in with Apple (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Apple, we only receive an anonymous user identifier from Apple to associate your in-app data. We do not obtain your real name or email unless you explicitly authorize it.

2.4 System Permissions

  • Photos (write): Used only when you choose to save or share an artwork image, to save the image to your photo library. We do not read other content in your library.

3. Protection of Minors

The App is intended for hobbyists of all ages and is suitable for parents and children to use together. The App is not intended for standalone use by children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We recommend parents enable Apple's "Ask to Buy" and "Screen Time" to manage in-app purchases. If you believe a child made a purchase without consent, you may request a refund through Apple's official channels.

4. Data Security and Retention

  • Your data is stored locally and protected by your device's system security.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes local data (purchases can be restored via "Restore Purchases").
  • We perform no cross-border data transfers (as data never leaves your device).

5. Your Rights

Because data is stored locally, you may at any time delete artworks, clear cache within the App, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Policy from time to time and will post the effective date within the App and on this page.

7. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, contact: flashbody@foxmail.com

In case of any discrepancy between the Chinese and English versions, the Chinese version prevails in mainland China and the English version prevails elsewhere.
